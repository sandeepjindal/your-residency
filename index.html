Stacking residencies — the part everyone gets wrong

You don’t “apply” for multiple tax residencies, you trigger them by days — UK Statutory Residence Test (183 days, or fewer with ties), India (182 days, or 120–181 days if Indian income > ₹15 lakh → RNOR), Thailand (180 days in a calendar year, Revenue Code s.41). PR stacking is the opposite problem: every PR has an absence clock, and the clocks fight each other. These three stacks are sequenced to respect the clocks in the research.

Most realistic dual

UK ILR → Canada PR hedge

  • Year 0 — Canada Express Entry: PR on arrival, ~6 months processing. The only PR in this set you don’t wait years for.
  • Years 0–5 — Live in the UK for ILR (≤180 days out in any rolling 12 months). ⚠️ UK Earned Settlement would move this to 10 years, or 3 years at ≥£125,141 — announced 2 Nov 2026, not yet law.
  • By Year 5 — You are 38 at UK ILR. Canada needs 730 days in Canada in every 5 years — you must bank Canada days before or after the UK block, not during it.

Conflict: UK ILR lapses after 2 years away; Canada lapses if you miss 730 days in 5. You cannot “park” both indefinitely — you have to keep returning to one of them.

Fastest in Europe

Germany Blue Card → EU long-term

  • Month 21–27 — Niederlassungserlaubnis with B1 German (21 mo) or A1 (27 mo; some sources say 33 mo without language — verify with BAMF).
  • Year 5 — German citizenship, dual citizenship now allowed (since 27 Jun 2024). The 3-year fast-track was repealed 30 Oct 2025.
  • Then — As an EU citizen you can live/work anywhere in the EU — this is the “UK ILR + Europe” idea, done via citizenship, not by holding two PRs at once.

German settlement lapses after ~6 months abroad unless a longer absence is approved in advance. Don’t leave for a year and assume it’s waiting.

High pay, high risk

US pay spike → return with capital

  • SF median $480k (~£358k), Meta $840k (~£627k) — Seattle $460k with no state income tax beats SF after tax.
  • Spouse: H-4 cannot work by default. Only L-1/L-2 (spouse works incident to status) or EB-1 fixes this.
  • Green card: EB-2 India final action date 1 Nov 2013 (Nov 2026 Visa Bulletin); ~179-year NFAP estimate for a Jan 2026 EB-2 filing.

Verdict in hindsight: the US only makes financial sense as a 3–5 year earnings spike on L-1A/EB-1 — not as a settlement plan for an Indian citizen on H-1B.

What the research actually recommends

🏆 Stay — if £240k holds

Stay in London if Meta keeps you at ~£240k+ and the Earned Settlement 3-year route for ≥£125k earners survives. It preserves both incomes, English schooling, and the fastest realistic settlement for this family. London nursery is ~£2,120/mo (Numbeo $2,844) — the cost you pay for not taking a 40–50% pay cut.

🇩🇪 Best all-round move — Berlin

Near-free Kita, married tax splitting (Ehegattensplitting), Kindergeld €255/mo/child (2025), 12–14 months Elterngeld, 5-year citizenship with dual nationality, fastest PR in Europe. The price is a ~40–50% gross cut vs. £240k and learning German to B1.

🇨🇦 Best long-term hedge — Toronto

Express Entry PR on arrival, citizenship after 1,095 days in 5 years, Cost of Living Index 67.5 vs. London 87.5, spouse open work permit (TEER 0/1 since 21 Jan 2025). EM median CA$228k (~£121k) is roughly half London — you are buying security, not pay.

Do not move to the US on H-1B or to Singapore on an EP if your wife working in tech is a hard requirement — the research shortlist is explicit on this. Singapore tax is the lowest here (~12–15% effective, no capital gains tax) but a Dependant’s Pass holder may not work on that pass (Letter of Consent withdrawn 1 May 2021) and foreign children mostly need international school at SGD 35,000–55,000/yr/child, which eats the tax saving.

How the calculator works — and what it leaves out

Formula (tap to collapse)

For each city: Cash left = EM median TC (£) × (1 − effective tax) − annual childcare. Projected net worth = your net worth today + (cash left × your savings share × horizon years). It does not compound investment returns, model rent (use the Cost of Living Index in each card as your guide — Zurich 118.5, Singapore 87.7, London 87.5, Toronto 67.5), model a second salary, or model RSU-by-country tax treatment. Married splitting (Germany), the Dutch 30% ruling (30% tax-free for up to 5 years, dropping to 27% from Jan 2027) and Spain’s Beckham Law (24% flat for 6 years) all move the real figure materially — they are noted on the cards, not baked into one false-precision number.

India, Thailand & the UK at the same time?

You cannot do 183 (UK) + 182 (India) + 180 (Thailand) = 545 days in one year. Triple tax residency only happens via low-day routes and different year-ends (UK Apr–Apr, India Apr–Mar, Thailand Jan–Dec) — e.g. 180 days Thailand + 120 days India (with Indian income >₹15 lakh) + ~65 days UK with enough SRT ties. Shields matter more than residency itself: UK FIG regime (first 4 years), India RNOR (foreign income sheltered), Thailand LTR exemption on remitted foreign income (Royal Decree; otherwise foreign income earned from 2024 is taxable when remitted, Por.161/2566 — pre-2024 income stays exempt, Por.162/2566). Without a shield, triple residency is triple filing with foreign tax credits, plus tie-breakers under the UK–India, UK–Thailand and India–Thailand treaties.

What does a monthly SIP actually grow to? Set the amount, return and years — with an optional annual step-up — and see the future value, what you put in, and what compounding added. Year by year, no black box.

The formula used — exactly as specified Future Value = P × ({[1 + r]^n – 1} / r) × (1 + r)

P = monthly SIP amount  •  r = monthly rate = annual return % ÷ 12 ÷ 100  •  n = total number of months = years × 12. The final × (1 + r) is the annuity-due adjustment — it assumes each instalment is paid at the start of the month. With a step-up above 0%, the same formula is applied year by year with that year’s higher P and the results are compounded together, which is why the table below is calculated month by month.

Estimate, not a guarantee: this assumes a smooth, constant return every month.
